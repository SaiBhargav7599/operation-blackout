01

Design Goal

My goal for this project was to bring a mix of stealth, action, and investigation — a believable story with emotional stakes like Splinter Cell and Metal Gear Solid 1, combined with narrative-driven spatial progression. I wanted every space to feel purposeful — not just visually distinct, but emotionally and mechanically meaningful.

The level is structured around a rising emotional arc:

Vulnerability → Tension → Loss → Hope → Empowerment

My core design goals focused on three interconnected pillars:

Use Environmental Storytelling to drive narrative progression
Provide Multiple Infiltration Routes to support player agency
Employ Escalating Spatial Hierarchy to reinforce emotional beats

Level Overview & Highlights

Setting & Tone

Setting: A remote forest concealing a clandestine enemy research facility

Tone & Mood: Isolation, tension, dread, rising urgency

Engine: UE5Third-Person ShooterPC

Synopsis

This level is set in a remote forest where a spec-ops squad is ambushed while approaching a hidden enemy research facility. The player — the sole survivor of the crash — must navigate hostile territory, uncover the truth behind the ambush, and sabotage the facility before the looming danger surfaces.

ACT 1 — Mission Objectives

Narrative-Driven Environment Storytelling

Video Overview
  • Used environmental storytelling to create a world that feels alive and reflects the enemy facility's current state.
  • Mixed in overheard enemy chatter, posters, warning signs, and a cracked mirror to give the world personality as the player explores.
  • Used environmental breadcrumbs — blood trails, stone debris, tree branches, bodies — to deepen immersion.

Rewarding Exploration

Video Overview
  • Placed hidden and visible rewards throughout to drive exploration and give players a sense of meaningful discovery.
  • 8 Interaction triggers · 4 Audio logs · 4 Ammo & 8 Medical bandage pickups
  • Positioned props to subtly guide players toward revealing secrets about the facility.

Enemy Types Overview

Video Overview

Any guard who detects the player immediately alerts the entire team — creating high-stakes tension and encouraging careful navigation.

Type 01

Standby Guards

Role: Static sentinels at key choke points. Maintain constant line of sight over important doors and corridors.

Encourage stealth takedowns · Reinforce a tightly controlled facility feel.

Type 02

Patrolling Guards

Role: Mobile threats along predefined patrol routes. Designed to catch players who rely on predictable patterns.

Add dynamism · Force players to track movement cycles.

Type 03

Enemy Workers

Role: Non-combat staff who still pose a detection risk. Unarmed but alert — report suspicious activity to guards.

Challenge awareness · Make the environment feel operational.

Supports Multiple Playstyles

  • Left Path — Collapsed Building: Tense, grounded route through debris. Lift a fallen door and slip through a fractured fence.

Techniques

Use Breadcrumbs to Guide Players

Scattered stone debris as a natural breadcrumb trail leading the player toward the hidden entrance.
Subtle blood traces on the floor guide attention toward the location of dead bodies and the next point of interest.
Fallen tree branches guide the player toward the intended pathway leading into the facility.

Points of Interest (POI)

A barren tree draws the player's eye amid surrounding greenery, while the yellow building and tower's blinking light serve as strong visual anchors.

Affordances

The flashing light toward the yellow edge communicates the building is climbable — using the same yellow convention many games use for traversal.
Same visual-guidance technique applied to a small environmental puzzle — pull the object close and use it as a step to progress.

Landmarks

A central structure anchors the player's orientation and serves as a consistent landmark — visible from anywhere in the environment, helping players maintain spatial awareness intuitively.

Environmental Storytelling

Lab space marked by bloodstains, fallen bodies, and prisoner graffiti — conveying a sudden violent outbreak.
Cramped dorm with scattered papers and a broken mirror showing stress and declining morale.
Sealed bunker entrance with warning signs, red lighting, and barricades suggesting emergency lockdown.
Monitoring station establishing the facility's hierarchy and surveillance culture.
Work zone with "Project Dawn" labels foreshadowing instability around the prototype.
Storage area with shift-reminder poster and audio-log terminal reinforcing rising security concerns.

Contrast & Composition

Light, shadow, and color are used to emphasise important gameplay areas or objects.

Spatial Composition

Additional side paths added to encourage exploration, offering loot or narrative elements.

Framing & Leading Lines

Highlighted stairs and directional edges form clear leading lines communicating the intended movement path.
Corridor's converging lines frame the bright exit, naturally pulling the player toward the open space.
Leading lines from path, terrain, and fencing guide the player's eye toward the command-centre entrance.
Terrain slopes and tree placement frame the structure, creating a clear visual funnel toward the tower.

3

First Roughout

Goal
Build a functional, testable version of the level as quickly as possible.
PureRef Board
  • Shifted 2D layout into UE5 and constructed environment using simple block-out geometry
  • Iterated in play mode to assess navigation, sightlines, and gameplay flow
  • Implemented placeholder enemies, NPCs, and loot to simulate intended gameplay structure
  • Used Level Design Kit for rapid prototyping and PureRef board for visual direction

Result: The level reached a fully playable state early. Core gameplay loop — exploration, reward discovery, story progression — was clearly communicated.

4

Iterating the Roughout

Goal
Transform the Crash Site and Perimeter Yard into a cohesive, immersive opening sequence.
  • Redesigned Crash Site to open with a short cinematic, panoramic reveal, and title card
  • Replaced cut-down fence with a small environmental puzzle supported by breadcrumbs
  • Introduced an early jump challenge using a broken cliff edge leading to the first enemy encounter
  • Refined lighting and sky atmosphere · Switched Game Mode to ALS

Result: The introduction now feels alive, intentional, and narratively cohesive.

5

Moving to the Blockout

Goal
Address spaces lacking spatial depth, player guidance, and purposeful interactions.
  • Introduced a tall landmark structure with distinctively coloured pillars as a visual anchor
  • Fixed a sequence break where players could miss key Commander Suite story beats
  • Expanded dormitory into two floors — Dorm A (golden path) and Dorm B (optional)
  • Redesigned the Armory for improved pacing, combat readability, and cover placement
6

Iterating the Blockout

Goal
Finalise tutorial encounter; polish Right-Side areas and Parking Lot; refine Briefing & Commander Suite; begin lighting and post-processing.
  • Moved first enemy encounter to cliff edge to give the player a breather
  • Reworked parking lot with stealth traversal props, attention-guiding lighting, and Blueprint interactions
  • Colour-coded non-interactive props in neutral grey to reduce visual noise
  • Introduced post-processing to reinforce an oppressive mood

Key Design Lessons

  • Emotional pacing is as important as mechanical pacing.
  • Environmental storytelling can carry narrative weight without cutscenes.
  • Spatial hierarchy naturally guides player expectations.
  • Every area should answer "Why does this exist?"

This project helped me refine my ability to blend narrative and gameplay into a cohesive experience.
